About TekMate

Engineering the
Future of Industry

TekMate Automation is a leading industrial automation company based in India, delivering smart technology solutions that enhance efficiency, reduce operational costs, and drive digital transformation for manufacturers across sectors.

From conceptual design to commissioning, our engineers work closely with clients to deploy tailor-made automation systems. We specialize in PLC/SCADA programming, electrical control panel fabrication, industrial IoT integration, and comprehensive after-sales support.

Our Mission

To empower industries through intelligent automation that maximizes productivity and ensures operational excellence.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted industrial automation partner, driving the next generation of smart manufacturing.
